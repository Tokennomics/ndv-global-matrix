## Abstract
Standard macroeconomic accounting via Gross Domestic Product (GDP) fails to account for thermodynamic energy constraints, ecosystem degradation, attention extraction, and systemic financialization. We present **Net Domestic Value (NDV)**, a first-principles sovereign accounting protocol that unifies thermodynamic exergy analysis, biological human decay, attention economy extraction, and material-weighted entropy transfers. Applied to 252 sovereign entities, NDV exposes massive structural wealth overstatements in post-industrial consumer economies while revealing hidden preservation value in natural sink nations. Econometric Granger causality testing (\(N=1,000\) Monte Carlo trials) demonstrates that NDV ratios predict long-term sovereign bond spreads and human flourishing (\(p < 0.001\)) with significantly higher precision than standard GDP.
